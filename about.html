~about-me

Who am I?

Hello, I'm Siddharth!

I'm a Computer Science master's student at RPTU Kaiserslautern, specialising in Intelligent Systems and Software Engineering. I work on AI safety from three directions: research, engineering and community.

At DFKI I help build Privacy Guardrail, an open-source browser extension that redacts personal data on-device before it reaches an LLM. My guided research is on multi-agent safety. I maintain FrontierKit, a library for comparing LLM setups with honest error bars, and I help organise AI Safety Kaiserslautern.

Before my master's I spent two years at HashedIn by Deloitte building backend systems, analytics pipelines and RAG chatbots, and interned at Microsoft on on-device image super-resolution.
